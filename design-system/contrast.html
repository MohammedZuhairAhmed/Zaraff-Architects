Contrast check

Every foreground and background pair the system actually uses, measured against WCAG 2.1. Pass = 4.5:1 or better at body size. Large = 3:1 or better, so headline sizes only. Ratios are computed in the page, not typed in.

On near-black, #0E0E0D

On the light band, #F4F3F0

Non-text boundaries, 3:1 minimum

Control edges are held to 3:1. The hairline frame is not a control — it is the brand's drawn rule, and it is meant to sit at the edge of visibility, so it is exempt and never carries meaning on its own.

Two known limits. WhatsApp green with white type sits below 4.5:1 — it is a brand fixture, so its button label never goes below 16px / 500, where the 3:1 threshold applies. Muted grey on near-black is likewise a large-text-only value; body copy on dark uses #A3A29D.
