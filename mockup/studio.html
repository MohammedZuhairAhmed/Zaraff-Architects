On site

Zaraff is a young practice that draws buildings and then stays to build them.

Most studios hand you a drawing set and wish you luck. We kept the site team because the gap between a drawing and a building is where projects go wrong — and because standing on site every week is the fastest way to learn what actually works.

We work mostly on private houses and interior fitouts around Hyderabad. We are small on purpose. It means the person who drew your plan is the person you meet on site.
