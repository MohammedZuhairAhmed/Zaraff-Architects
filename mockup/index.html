What a builder does not give you

  1. 01

    A measured drawing set

    Plans, elevations, sections and details, drawn to scale. Not a sketch on site, adjusted as the wall goes up.

  2. 02

    The house in 3D before it exists

    Renders and 360° walkthroughs from the working model. You approve a house you have already walked through.

  3. 03

    Municipal sanction, handled

    The sanction set prepared and filed by us. You do not stand in a queue with a rolled-up drawing.

  4. 04

    Construction against that drawing

    Our own site team building the thing that was drawn, with weekly photographs whether you ask or not.

  5. 05

    One person accountable

    When the drawing and the building disagree, there is nobody else to blame. That is the whole point.
